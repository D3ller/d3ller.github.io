SharedStats is a Paper plugin for Minecraft 26.2. Players join a group, and once a game starts, their health, food level and potion effects are synchronized: when one player takes damage or gets hungry, everyone in the group does too.

Saturation stays per player on purpose. Since every player's hunger drains the same food bar, food runs out much faster than in solo play, so the group has to coordinate.

Each stat can be turned on or off, and a death leaderboard tracks who brings the group down most often. The plugin is available on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/bukkit-plugins/sharedstats) and [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/plugin/sharedstats).
