TableKit is a SaaS for restaurants. Staff manage tables, menus, bookings and the floor plan from an admin dashboard, and customers book through a public widget. Each restaurant is its own tenant.

- **Front end**: Nuxt 4 with Nuxt UI, Pinia, Tailwind, i18n and Konva for the floor plan editor.
- **Back end**: AdonisJS 7 with Lucid, VineJS validation, Bouncer policies and live updates over Server-Sent Events.
- **Infrastructure**: PostgreSQL with read replicas, Cloudflare R2 for files, Resend for emails, Cloudflare Turnstile against bots, Axiom for logs and PostHog for analytics.
