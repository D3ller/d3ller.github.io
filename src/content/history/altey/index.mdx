Altey helped with job hunting. You filled in one or more profiles and your education, saved the companies you wanted to apply to, and Altey wrote a cover letter for each one with AI, then sent the application from your own Gmail account.

- **Front end**: Nuxt, with a dashboard for applications, companies, profiles and integrations.
- **Back end**: AdonisJS, with queued jobs for letter generation, emails and quotas.
- **AI**: GPT-5 mini through the Vercel AI SDK.
- Google sign-in and Gmail integration, Bunny CDN for files, and Docker for deployment.
