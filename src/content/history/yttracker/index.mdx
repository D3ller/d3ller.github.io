ytTracker kept track of the YouTube videos you watched and turned them into stats, the way Spotify tells you which artists you listen to most. It showed your most-watched videos and channels for today or another period, plus your recent history.

<video src="/history/yttracker/demo.mp4" controls muted playsinline></video>

## How it worked

1. A Chrome extension detects each video you open on YouTube and sends its ID to the API, authenticated with your token.
2. The API, built with ElysiaJS on Bun, fetches the video's title, thumbnail and channel, then stores it in PostgreSQL through Prisma.
3. A Nuxt front end displays the stats.

I never fully finished it.
