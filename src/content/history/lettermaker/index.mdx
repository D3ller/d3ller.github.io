LetterMaker generated cover letters with AI. You filled in a form about yourself and the job, picked a model, GPT-4o or DeepSeek, and got a letter you could export as PDF, DOCX or plain text. It also had a dedicated form for Parcoursup applications.

<video src="/history/lettermaker/demo.mp4" controls muted playsinline></video>

## Dashboard

The dashboard showed how many letters you had generated and saved, and your recent letters. You could follow each application with a status: not sent, sent, rejected, interview or hired. A usage page showed how much of your monthly quota was left.

## Stack

- **Nuxt 3** for both the front end and the API, with Tailwind, GSAP and SplitType for the landing page animations.
- **OpenAI SDK** for both models, since DeepSeek exposes an OpenAI-compatible API.
- **Prisma** on PostgreSQL, with session-based auth.
- **Stripe** for subscriptions: each plan has a monthly letter limit, with a checkout page, a customer portal and webhooks.
- **PostHog** for analytics, sitemap, robots and JSON-LD for SEO, and nuxt-security.
- Cypress tests, a GitHub Actions build with Bun, and PM2 to run it in production.
