A portfolio website for MX Flash, a photographer, videographer and video editor based in Troyes. It presents the photo and video work and the pricing packs, and has a contact form protected by Cloudflare Turnstile.

Clients get a private gallery link to view their photos. The content is managed with Eponyme, and media are served through Cloudinary.

Built with Nuxt, Tailwind and Prisma on Neon.
