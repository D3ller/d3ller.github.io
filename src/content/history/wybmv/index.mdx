A small site made for Valentine's Day. You create a page with the person's name, a message and an optional picture, then send them the link. They answer yes or no, and you get the result on your own page, with confetti if it's a yes.

Built with Nuxt, Prisma on PostgreSQL, GSAP and canvas-confetti.
